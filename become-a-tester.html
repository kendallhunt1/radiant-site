Tester program

Want to become a tester? Apply here.

We're building an early tester group to help shape how radiant feels, performs, and grows before wider rollout.

Benefits

  • Free premium for life.
  • Get the newest versions first.
  • Unlimited groups in the community.
  • Free AI usage for life.
  • Priority access to new experiments and unreleased features.
  • Collaborate with other testers and radiant to shape how it's built.

Who We Want

  • People who actively log their health already (nearly everyday).
  • People who care about their health but don't currently log it.
  • People who have used health logging apps in the past or present.
  • People willing to join and contribute to communities.
  • People who can give clear, practical product feedback.

What's Expected

  • Use radiant consistently enough to form real opinions.
  • Share bugs, friction points, and ideas while testing.
  • Be responsive when we ask for specific product feedback.
  • Download and use new versions as they become available.
  • Participate thoughtfully if you choose to join community testing spaces.
  • Health professionals are expected to use the pro tools, including groups, plans, client logs, health metrics, and the radiant CRM, and provide feedback on them.

Verification required for CRM access

Prove your professional status

Radiant CRM includes professional tools for managing clients, schedules, plans, logs, and health metrics. To help protect clients and keep professional access limited to qualified testers, we verify every CRM applicant.

Submit one of these

  • A current professional or clinical license.
  • A degree or diploma in your stated health field.
  • A current certification from a recognized issuing organization.
  • An employer verification letter on official letterhead confirming your health-related role.
  • An official registry record showing your name, credential, and active status.

These do not qualify by themselves

  • A résumé or CV.
  • A social media profile, website, or personal bio.
  • A course receipt, class schedule, or unfinished training.
  • A document unrelated to the profession selected below.
  • An unreadable, cropped, expired, or altered document.

Your CRM application will be denied if the documentation does not clearly support your claimed health professional status. You may still be considered for mobile-app-only testing.

The document must be legible and show your name, professional credential or role, and issuing organization. It must be current where an expiration date applies. Accepted formats: PDF, PNG, JPG, DOC, or DOCX.

What counts as a health professional?

Check this box if you work professionally in a health-related role or directly support other people's health, wellness, or care.

This can include roles like physician, nurse, dietitian, therapist, health coach, fitness professional, personal trainer, or other clinical or wellness practitioners.

CRM access requires documentation that directly verifies the profession you claim. A résumé, social profile, personal website, or unrelated certificate is not sufficient by itself.

If you cannot provide qualifying documentation, leave this box unchecked and apply as a mobile app tester. Applications without verifiable professional documentation will be denied CRM access.
